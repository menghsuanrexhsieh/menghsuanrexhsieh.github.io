Welcome to the new site. This first post exists mostly to show how writing
works here — and to give the search box something to find.

## How to add a post

Create a Markdown file in the `_posts/` folder named with the date and a slug:

```
_posts/2026-06-10-hello-world.md
```

Add front matter at the top with a title, date, and any tags, then write the
body in Markdown. That's it — push to GitHub and the post appears.

## What goes here

Short notes, longer essays, reading lists — whatever you want to keep. Each
post can carry **tags**, which power the filter buttons and feed into search.

> Tip: keep titles descriptive. Both titles and full post content are indexed
> by the search box on the Posts page.

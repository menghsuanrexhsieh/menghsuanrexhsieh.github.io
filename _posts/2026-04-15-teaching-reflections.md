The semester wrapped up last week. A few things I want to remember for next
time, while they're still fresh.

## Office hours beat lectures

The most useful moments were almost always one-on-one. When a student brings a
specific confusion, you can meet them exactly where they are — something a
lecture can't do for thirty people at once.

## Worked examples, slowly

Students consistently asked for more fully worked examples done at a deliberate
pace. It feels slow in the moment, but the payoff on assignments was clear.

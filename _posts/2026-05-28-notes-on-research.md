A few habits I keep coming back to when a new project starts. None of this is
novel, but writing it down helps me actually do it.

## Start with the question

Before any code or experiments, I try to write the central question in one
sentence. If I can't, the project isn't scoped yet. The sentence goes at the
top of a running notes file and gets revised as understanding improves.

## Keep a lab notebook

A dated, append-only log of what I tried and what happened. Cheap to maintain,
invaluable three months later when I can't remember why I abandoned an idea.

1. What did I try?
2. What did I expect?
3. What actually happened?

That's enough structure to make the notes useful without making them a chore.
